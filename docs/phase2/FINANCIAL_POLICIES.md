# Financial integrity and policy decision register

This register separates **observed MVP behavior**, **user-required invariants**, and **proposed policy requiring A/B signoff**. Date: 10 October 2026. Documentation publication does not approve any accounting policy. No decision below is represented as an accounting, tax or religious certification.

## Preserved baseline and mandated boundaries

Observed source: monthly snapshots hold cash movements separately from revenue/expense and EOM balances; they are not an accrual or double-entry ledger. `MonthlyRecordService` replaces manual snapshots, adds explicitly confirmed document flows and recalculates the affected month plus up to five later recorded months. COGS/AR/AP/inventory/loan/interest can be missing. Code uses `BigDecimal`, `numeric(14,2)` persisted money, and legacy numeric JSON without an explicit currency field. Full evidence is in [financial audit](audit/FINANCIAL_DATA.md).

Preserve `health-score-v1`: weights 30/25/20/15/10, dynamic availability normalization, industry thresholds, score bands, declared repayment/compliance and historical evidence behavior. Existing null-COGS formula treatment is a **known methodology limitation**, not permission to substitute zero for unknown COGS in new analytics. Existing Zakat `HANAFI_PK_BUSINESS_V1` 1.0.0 remains a separate preview with its own inputs and disclosures; inventory book value must not silently replace selling valuation.

User-required invariants: deterministic backend calculations; one owner per authoritative metric; no fabricated historical transactions; no double counting; no financial posting from estimates or AI; explicit unknown values; tenant isolation; audited/reversible financial corrections; preserve migrations and existing functionality. Attachment additionally requires no cash at invoice issue, weighted-average inventory costing, permission/module precedence and read-only first AI release. These high-level requirements do not decide tax, timing, valuation exceptions or rounding.

## Reconciliation identities to freeze as fixtures

These are proposed test identities, subject to approved classifications. They do not silently settle the open timing decisions.

* Account ending balance = opening + accepted signed movements; internal transfers conserve business total and create no revenue/expense.
* Obligation outstanding = recognized principal − valid credits − allocated payments; unallocated advance tracked separately, not negative invoice outstanding by default.
* Project actual cost = sum of unique approved source allocations, including recognized stock/sample/commission costs once. Sum(project allocations)+unallocated remainder = allocatable source amount.
* Stock closing value = opening + accepted receipt/return/adjustment value − approved issue value, with explicitly assigned rounding residual. Sum of movements explains valuation.
* Settlement net cash = gross cash − fees; realized FX compares gross settlement with released historical carrying amount using direction-specific signs, not net cash alone.
* Projection rebuild from the same event watermark = stored monthly projection. Only ledger-month projections feed ledger-month scores; no additive merge with a manual snapshot.
* Report, widget and AI tool for identical scope/period/revision must equal the domain endpoint. A unavailable metric stays unavailable across every surface.
